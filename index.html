Places to go

You spawn in your dorm. Open your watch, press Play, and head out.

The dorm

A loft with a desk and ladder, a bed, windows, and a mirror that really shows your reflection.

Rec SandBox

The Rec Center

The main hall with the big sign, three tiers, an upstairs with stairs, and a door to the cafeteria.

The cafeteria

Grab food from the machine, pull up a chair, and sit with friends.

Crash Zone

A big playground of ramps, crates, and barrels. Smash through it and watch your car dent.

Make it yours

Build rooms, dress your player, and play together.

Maker Pen

Open your backpack and place cubes, balls, ramps, pizza, burgers, and more. Your creations are saved in each room.

Customize your player

Pick your skin, shirt, pants, and hat. Your look is saved with your account.

Flat screen or VR

Play with a keyboard and mouse, or put on a headset and use your hands. In VR your avatar follows your head and controllers.

Your watch

Press Y to open your watch. Check your profile, press Play to travel, or open your Backpack.

Real car damage

Hits slow you down, pull you to one side, and knock parts off. Press F to repair.

Accounts that stay

Create an account once and stay signed in. Your look and creations are waiting next time.

Controls

Everything you need on day one.

Action Flat screen VR
Move and jump W A S D and Space Left stick walk, right stick snap-turn
Open the watch Y Left controller Y button
Maker Pen Click to place, right-click to delete, R to rotate, wheel to resize Right trigger place, A delete, B rotate, right stick resize
Drive in Crash Zone W / S gas and brake, A / D steer, Space drift Right trigger gas, left trigger brake, left stick steer
See yourself V for selfie view, or E at the mirror Look in the dorm mirror

Get RecSandbox

Free fan game made in Godot 4.3 or newer. Unzip, open the folder in Godot, and press F5.

Flat screen

Play with a keyboard and mouse on a PC or Mac.

  1. Unzip RecSandbox.zip
  2. Import project.godot in Godot
  3. Press F5 and create an account

Download Flat Screen Build

VR

For Meta Quest 3 and Pro, or a PC headset through SteamVR or Oculus.

  1. Unzip RecSandboxVR.zip
  2. Import it in Godot
  3. Start your headset, then press F5
